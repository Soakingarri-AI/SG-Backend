---
title: Queen Amina of Zazzau
era: 16th century CE
region: Hausaland (present-day Nigeria)
---

Amina of Zazzau (Zaria) was a Hausa warrior queen who ruled the city-state of
Zazzau in the 16th century. Renowned as a formidable military strategist, she
expanded Zazzau's territory to its greatest extent, opening and securing trade
routes across the Sahel.

Amina is traditionally credited with commissioning the earthen defensive walls
built around military camps and cities, some of which came to be known as "ganuwar
Amina" (Amina's walls). Her campaigns brought neighbouring peoples under Zazzau's
influence and channelled the kola nut and other trade toward the city.

She stands in Hausa oral tradition as a symbol of leadership, independence, and
military skill, and remains a widely celebrated figure in Nigerian history.
